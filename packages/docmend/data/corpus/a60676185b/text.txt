## The method

- 01

### Assess

Map the real workflows first. Where hours actually go, not where the demo looks good.

- 02

### Pilot

One team, one measurable workflow. Working tool over slideware, every time.

- 03

### Train

Playbooks people actually open. Meet them at their level and remove the fear.

- 04

### Measure

Adoption is a number, not a vibe. Usage, time saved, and whether it stuck.
