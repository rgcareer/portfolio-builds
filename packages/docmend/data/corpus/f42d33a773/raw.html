About

I help people adopt new technology. I've done it for twenty years. Now it's AI.

I'm Ryan. For about twenty years, across retail, enterprise tech, and SaaS, my work has come down to the same thing: helping people who aren't technical get comfortable with new tools. AI is that job again, and the stakes are higher this time.

The throughline

Looking back, every job I've had was really the same problem: getting people to adopt something new.

  1. 2004-2015
    Retail management · Zumiez · Vans · Mainland

    Ran teams of 15 to 35 and set up the hiring, onboarding, and training that got new people up to speed.

  2. 2015-2019
    Enterprise technology adoption · Apple

    Helped organizations like St. Luke’s and Dutch Bros move onto new tools, trained more than 22 teammates, and brought NPS from 83 to 92.

  3. 2019-2022
    SaaS sales & enablement · Cradlepoint · Intuit · Vacasa

    Moved small businesses onto cloud and SaaS tools, with work on CRM cleanup, onboarding, and training that held up.

  4. Current
    AI product builder · Smart Business AI

    Build AI products for non-technical business owners, and handle the whole thing myself from research to support.

The part that's rare

I lead change

Twenty years getting people and teams through new tools and processes: the training, the onboarding, the buy-in conversations. This is the human side of adoption, and it's usually where a rollout lives or dies.

And I build

I build the AI I talk about. Prompt engineering, workflow automation, an MCP server, a job-search engine, a multi-agent review system. See the work

The useful part is the combination.

I can run the adoption side and build the tools, and most people only do one.

Where I'm headed

I'm looking for roles where that combination matters: AI enablement and adoption, forward-deployed or solutions engineering, customer success, or AI training, at companies that take AI seriously. Forward-deployed work is the shape I already fit: sit with the customer, build the tool that matches how they actually work, and stay until it sticks. My AI title is new, and I'm fine with that. I'd rather show the work than pad a résumé.
